# CloudNova Technologies — Demonstration HR Policy (Fictional)
Version: 1.0 | Training document only | Effective: 1 November 2026

## Working hours
The regular work week is Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:30, including a 30-minute lunch break.
Employees should use the company attendance portal to report their arrival and departure.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year, subject to supervisor approval.
Leave requests of five days or longer should be submitted at least ten working days in advance.
Unused leave is subject to a maximum carryover of five days, with HR approval.

## Remote work
Staff may request up to two work-from-home days per week when approved by their line manager.
Employees handling confidential customer data must use the company VPN and approved devices.

## Information security
Never share passwords, MFA recovery codes or client credentials in email or chat.
Report a suspected security incident to the IT Help Desk within 30 minutes of discovery.

## Support contacts
HR enquiries: hr@cloudnova.example (fictional address).
IT issues: support@cloudnova.example (fictional address).

This document is a fictional teaching example. It must not be treated as an actual KICS or Huawei policy.
